## 2. The 6 Architectural Pillars of the New `10/10` `TitanIntro` Suite (All 54 Themes)

Every single one of the **54 `intro_{id}.js` modules** (`~17.5–19.3 KB` each, nearly `1 MB` of bespoke GLSL ES 1.00 & JS choreography) has been rebuilt from the ground up with:

1. **Dual-Channel `1024×768` Physical Relief Texture (`TX`) + 5-Tap Sobel Normal Perturbation**:
   - **Red Channel (`TX.r`)**: Wide Gaussian-beveled heightfield (`shadowBlur = 18px`) sampled via a 5-tap finite-difference Sobel filter (`hL, hR, hD, hU`) in the fragment shader to construct a true 3D surface normal `vec3 N = normalize(vec3((hL - hR) * 5.8, (hD - hU) * 5.8, 1.0))` and directional contact drop-shadow (`shUV`).
   - **Green Channel (`TX.g`)**: Unblurred, razor-sharp typography & filigree mask so every serif of `TITAN RELIQUARY` is pin-sharp while its bevels catch a real travelling GGX microfacet specular sweep (`t = 1.7s -> 3.6s`).
2. **4 World-Matched 3D Architectural Framing Modes (Zero Opaque Pasteboard Boxes on Open Worlds)**:
   - **Mode 0 — Open Monumental 3D Chiseled Relief** (`afterhours`, `glacier`, `abyss`, `supercell`, `canopy`, `saltflat`, `lightwell`, `obsidian`, `sahel`, `aurora`, `campfire`, `freefall`, `zen`, `contour`, `papercut`): **Zero box border**—monumental 3D chiselled letters float directly in the 3D scene so the columns, ice pillars, ocean caustics, tornado vortex, and salt mirror remain 100% unobstructed.
   - **Mode 1 — 3D Circular Oculus / Astrolabe / Watch Bezel** (`colossus`, `odyssey`, `kaleido`, `xeno`, `solaris`, `alchemist`, `samadhi`, `y2k`, `tourbillon`, `orbit`, `steampunk`, `glasshouse`): Framed by a 3D circular bezel ring with translucent depth.
   - **Mode 2 — 3D Pointed / Coffered Sanctuary Arch** (`valhalla`, `dynasty`, `silkroad`, `cursedwing`, `cathedral`, `roman`, `fireside`, `nocturne`, `diorama`): Framed by a 3D pointed or coffered arch with translucent sanctuary backing.
   - **Mode 3 — Translucent Engraved Plate / Screen / Print** (`conservator`, `notepad`, `construct`, `neon`, `arcade`, `pulp`, `drafting`, `bauhaus`, `terminal`, `marbled`, `banknote`, `sketch`, `privatebank`, `darkroom`, `silverscreen`, `noir`, `tarot`, `cassette`): Framed by a 3D beveled sheet/screen where the physical rectangular artifact is the subject.
3. **3D Reeded-Edge Classical Numismatic Specimen Medallion (`renderCoin3D`)**:
   - Features `140` milled edge reeds on the 3D cylinder rim, raised border rim, `96` beaded denticles, classical laurel wreath, domain-warped sovereign bust/crest relief, and anisotropic radial cartwheel mint luster.
   - Executes a 3D entry flip during Acts 1–2 and locks into a **heroic 3/4 perspective** (`abs(tiltY) <= 0.26 rad`) so it never turns edge-on during the reveal.
4. **True 3D Perspective Camera Dolly (`ro`, `rd`) & Damped Mechanical Impact Shake**:
   - Smooth cubic camera dolly (`dolly = mix(1.28, 1.0, smoothstep(0.0, 3.6, t))`) plus damped high-frequency camera shake (`exp(-max(0.0, t - tImpact) * 14.0)`) at the exact millisecond the theme's mechanism strikes or locks.
5. **Physical GGX Microfacet BRDF + Schlick Fresnel + ACES Filmic Tone Mapping + Dithered Grain**:
   - Dark physical metallic diffuse floor (`0.01–0.08`) with true Trowbridge-Reitz GGX normal distribution, Smith geometric self-shadowing, and ACES Filmic tone mapping.
6. **Multi-Layered 2D Canvas Fallback + Recorded Audio Choreography (`warm`/`cut`)**:
   - Every module provides a complete 2D Canvas fallback and recorded audio integration (`audio/ambience/{track}.webm|.m4a` and `audio/cuts/organ`).
