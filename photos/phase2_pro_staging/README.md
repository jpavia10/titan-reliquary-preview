### Capture Technical Specifications
- **Camera Rig**: 1:1 True Optical Macro Lens (90mm–105mm focal length recommended).
- **Lighting**: Dual 45-degree polarized continuous diffuse LED illumination with axial beam-splitter ring for cartwheel mint luster.
- **Die Alignment**:
  - Obverse: 0° upright orientation (`_obv`).
  - Reverse: 180° coin turn (or 0° medal turn depending on mint spec) (`_rev`).
- **File Format**: 16-bit uncompressed TIFF (`.tif`) or RAW DNG (`.dng`) Master; 2048px WebP / JPEG companion.
- **Color Profile**: Adobe RGB (1998) or ProPhoto RGB calibrated against X-Rite ColorChecker.
