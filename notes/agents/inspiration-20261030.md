## The new experimental UI: "The Field" is taken, so this one is "Constellation"

The collection as a living particle form over a ripple floor (rendered on TitanFXK like the theme worlds, so it is code, never film):
1. Open: the dust gathers into the featured coin (sampled from its real photo), then the collection name forms and lets go.
2. Scroll (or swipe on a phone) re-forms the particles: the coin -> the globe of 51 countries (each a cluster sized by its coin count, the
   country names as glass pills orbiting it) -> an album page (the holes as rings, filled ones bright) -> the value as a rising column.
3. Tap a pill: the particles fly into that country's coins, each a small cut-out photo; tap a coin: the object breakdown (layers + facts).
4. The ripple floor answers every touch with a ring; sound follows the theme's soundscape.
Same rules as The Cabinet: its own shell (`shells/constellation/`), the real data, every fact with its certainty, Calm/Off respected, the
phone first. It comes after the theme worlds are all on the engine and the Atmosphere is settled.
