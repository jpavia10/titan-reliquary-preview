# The Theme Pot (integrator plan, 2026-10-01)

Owner brief: themes are a personal feature he loves (with the ambient sound and music). Nothing is permanent. Reconsider all 20: analyze,
consolidate, rename, re-vibe, take creative liberty. Rebuild one at a time ("pull from the pot"), each from the ground up.

## Principles
1. **A theme is a World.** One manifest = color/type tokens (no layout overrides) + photoreal hero art (Blender) + a GPU effect preset
   (TitanFX) + a recorded-ambience scene (TitanGen) + a music mood (later). Picking a World sets all of it; the Sound tab can override.
2. **Scenes and themes become one idea.** Today "Scenes" (Quiet study, Rainy archive…) and "Lighting" (20 atmospheres) overlap and
   confuse. Worlds replace both; the 8-12 sound scenes survive as each World's default ambience and as "Sound only" presets.
3. **Tie every World to coins.** Places money was made, kept, lost, traded or studied. That is what makes it this app, not a skin pack.
4. **Signature vs Classic.** Rebuilt Worlds are "Signature". Old ones stay as "Classic" until replaced; retired when the new one ships.
5. **Stable ids.** Keep the old `data-atmo` id when a World descends from an old theme, so saved preferences carry over.
6. **One utility theme.** "Clear" (from notepad): maximum contrast, no effects, large type. Dad's daytime default. Not a World.
