# Short cuts from recorded music: sources and licenses

Short excerpts of real recordings for openings (`js/intros/`), cut once with ffmpeg from a recording that is already in `audio/music/`
(see `audio/music/CREDITS.md` for the full piece). Each is the length given, faded out at the end, AAC in M4A (96 kbps) and Opus in WebM
(80 kbps). Cached offline like the ambience (sw.js, cache-first). No generated audio here: every cut is a performance.
