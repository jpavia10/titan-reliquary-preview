UI proposals, round 2

From the design homework (docs/design/REDESIGN_BRIEF.md). Each prototype reads the real collection files; nothing here changes the app. Joseph picks what gets built, in small steps, behind the Motion setting.

The Field meets the rules

Grok, 2026-10-14

One table instead of five wings: rows are countries grouped by continent, left to right is the year, coins of the same country and year stack up. Dim coins still need a phone photo. Tap a coin: its row and year light up, the camera comes down and the coin lifts out with its photo, story and certainty labels. Back puts it home again. "List & search" does the same without gestures.

Not built yet: album, metal and to-photograph filters. Tested on a phone-sized screen in software graphics; not yet on a real phone (add ?fps to see the frame rate).

The Continuum sent back

Gemini, 2026-10-14

Six "lenses" in a bottom dock over one 3D stage (reserve, field, coin, atlas, albums, lab). Not hosted: it carries a frozen copy of the collection and hard-coded numbers instead of reading the live files, and it invents figures (a dealer, melt and pawn "liquidation" price list, metal ring pitches). The brief rejects both. Gemini has the list of fixes; the lens dock and the coin-settle animation are worth seeing again once it reads the real data.
