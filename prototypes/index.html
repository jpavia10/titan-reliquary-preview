Three new looks for the Hall

Each page below is a finished prototype of the home screen, built from the real ledger numbers. None keeps the old navy and gold look, the old crest or the old icon. Open each one on your phone, then pick the one you like.

Direction 1

Private Bank

Quiet luxury, the way a private bank or a fine watchmaker would show a client what they own. One large serif number on deep ink, ivory pages, hairline rules and a single restrained gold accent. Nothing glows, nothing moves unless it has to. It is the calmest and easiest to read, and the best fit for a collection that is held, not traded.

Direction 2

Product Page

Cinematic and bold, like a launch page for a flagship phone. A huge lit silver coin and a giant value open the page, then each section tells one story as you scroll: value, metals weighed ounce by ounce, a map of 45 countries, the Silver Eagle years still missing, and photo progress. The coin art slot is ready for the Blender render.

Direction 3

Modern Fintech

A crisp dashboard in the spirit of the best finance apps. Everything is on one screen of tidy cards: the total, metals with melt values, real charts of value, countries and album gaps, and a search palette. Dark by default with a light switch. It has no price chart on purpose, because the ledger has no price history to draw.
