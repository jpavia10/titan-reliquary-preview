# UI registry (plain words)

| UI | Commit | What it is | How to remove it |
|---|---|---|---|
| Classic | (original) | The wings: Hall, Gallery, Vault, Study, Lab. Safe default. | Not removable; every other UI falls back to it. |
| The Field | see git log "The Field as a UI" | Everything on one 3D table: rows are countries by continent, year runs across, equal coins stack. Tap a coin and the camera flies to it. Filters: album holes as gold rings, silver & gold, still to photograph. "Hold it" opens the full coin page with the photo-relief coin. | `git revert` that commit, or delete `shells/field/`, its entry in `shells/shell.js` and its lines in `sw.js`. |
| The Cabinet | see git log "The Cabinet" (Claude) | Every coin as its own photo cutout at its true relative size (catalogue diameter), lying in velvet trays. Lenses re-sort the same coins and each one glides to its new pocket: Country, Year, Value (estimate under each coin), New (by week added), Photos (the blank planchets are the shoot list, with Scan a coin) and Albums (each album a page of real holes: filled ones hold a coin in its metal, empty ones show the price to fill them). Tap a coin and it lifts out of its pocket: drag or tap to turn it over, the light follows your finger or the phone's tilt, every fact carries how sure we are and, on a tap, who said it, when and the source; Full record opens Classic's coin page with the photo-relief coin. Search lights the matching coins (or album holes); Enter lifts the first. Contract test `tools/smoke/test_regress_cl_cab01.py`. Pauses Classic's GPU effect and skips painting the wings while it is up. | `git revert` that commit, or delete `shells/cabinet/`, its `SHELLS` line in `shells/shell.js`, its 2 lines in `sw.js` and its smoke step. |

## Experimental (Grok exp worker; each lives on its own branch `ui/<id>` and is one squashed commit on main)
| UI | Branch | What it is | How to remove it |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hologram (`?ui=hologram`) | `ui/hologram` | A real-time GPU particle simulation (1M points on phone/tablet, 4M on a big desktop screen): curl-noise vortices, touch-stirred flow, HDR bloom, RGB fringe and scanlines. Your collection is a ring of light around you (year runs around the ring, metal sets the colour); tap one and every particle spirals in to build that coin from its photo plus a light panel for its facts. Back bursts it home. | `git revert` the commit "Experimental UI: Hologram", or delete `shells/hologram/`, its `SHELLS` line in `shells/shell.js` and its 3 lines in `sw.js`; then `git push origin --delete ui/hologram`. (`shells/exp/` is the shared kit for all experiments: remove it only with the last one.) |
| Orrery (`?ui=orrery`) | `ui/orrery` | A brass orrery floating in space. The sun is today; every decade is an orbit, so older coins circle farther out and slower (Kepler's law). Each coin is a lit metal disc wearing its own photo. 500k dust particles drift with the orbits (shared fx/ curl noise), with HDR bloom. Drag to swing round, pinch to zoom, the clock button sets the speed of time. Tap a coin and time stops, the coin lifts and turns to face you (under 0.7 s). | `git revert` the commit "Experimental UI: Orrery", or delete `shells/orrery/`, its `SHELLS` line and its 2 lines in `sw.js`; then `git push origin --delete ui/orrery`. |
| Deck (`?ui=deck`) | `ui/deck` | Every coin is an ivory foil card (photo, country, year, value pips, a suit by continent). Fan: flick through a springy fan of the whole deck, the centre card turns face up, tap it for its facts. Reading: shuffle and deal three cards from any suit (The Elder = oldest, The Treasure = most valuable, The Wanderer = random); tap to turn, tap again for facts. Foil sheen follows your finger/tilt. DOM + CSS 3D only, so it's light on the GPU. | `git revert` the commit "Experimental UI: Deck", or delete `shells/deck/`, its `SHELLS` line and its 2 lines in `sw.js`; then `git push origin --delete ui/deck`. |
| Sonic (`?ui=sonic`) | `ui/sonic` | Your collection as an instrument. Every coin is a glowing note on a dark field: higher up = newer and a higher pitch, left to right = where it is from (Americas to Asia/Oceania, panned the same way in your ears), the metal sets the tone (silver bright, gold mellow). Drag a finger across and every coin you pass rings; each strike sends a shock ring through 400k GPU particles (shared fx/ curl noise, HDR bloom). Tap a coin and the camera leans in (~0.55 s), the coin rings long and appears wearing its photo while its sourced facts rise. | `git revert` the commit "Experimental UI: Sonic", or delete `shells/sonic/`, its `SHELLS` line and its 2 lines in `sw.js`; then `git push origin --delete ui/sonic`. |
| Ask (`?ui=ask`) | `ui/ask` | Chat with your collection. Type a question or tap a suggestion ("how many silver coins", "oldest coins from Germany", "most valuable gold", "coins from the 1960s", "what's it all worth", "which need a photo"). A fixed parser reads only the published data/index.json (no AI guessing). Every answer names the file it used and shows the coins behind it; tap one for who checked each fact. Behind the chat, every coin is a light in a slow GPU galaxy (200k curl-noise dust, HDR bloom); the cited coins fly together into a joined-up constellation (~0.5 s) while the rest dim. Back clears it. | `git revert` the commit "Experimental UI: Ask", or delete `shells/ask/`, its `SHELLS` line and its 2 lines in `sw.js`; then `git push origin --delete ui/ask`. |
