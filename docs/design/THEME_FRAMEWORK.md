# The theme framework (locked 2026-10-29; every theme a photograph since 2026-11-08)

Owner, 2026-10-29: "get the framework for all of the themes locked so i can just improve piece by piece." Owner, 2026-11-08: "I
explicitly didnt want 3d worlds because your implementation is poor ... I asked for you to have my slaves make super high quality third
spaces as images then layering on it to get 3d effects or other features is a better use of resources"; "For example supercell and
shipwreck and midnight gallery the performance is so bad for how 1990s the quality is. Do it for all 50." (First said 2026-10-30:
the code-drawn rooms "look like low-quality 3D and run poorly on a phone".) Rule 12 in `CLAUDE.md` says what a theme must be; this page
says how it is built. Every theme fills the same slots, so any one piece (its photograph, its live layers, its sound, its music) can be
improved later without touching the others. Where each theme stands: `docs/themes/FRAMEWORK_STATUS.md` (generated by
`tools/themes/framework_status.py`).

**No code-built worlds.** Nobody writes a world in code or tunes an old one. The world files in `fx/` stay in the repository only as
history; `fx/kit.js` loads none of them (`K.worlds = false`, `K.lazy = {}`).

## 1. The parts

| Part | What it is |
|---|---|
| **The photograph engine** (`wings/plate.js`, `TitanPlate`) | One WebGL2 canvas (`#tr-plate`, z 0, behind the app) for every theme: the theme's photograph with the app's live layers on it (relief parallax on its depth, the light mask's flicker, glow and shafts, aurora, caustics or lightning where the room has them, particles in up to three depths, film grain). The same canvas is the backdrop, the opening, the room and the Atmosphere's live view. |
| The photographs (`art/rooms/{id}/`) | From the image AIs through the art queue (rule 9) as layer sets: `back.webp` (the room without its nearest object), `front.webp` (that object, green keyed out), `map.webp` (R = depth, filled in under the object; G = light), made by `tools/rooms/prep_plates.py`. Until a theme's set comes in, its own picture (`art/themes/{id}.webp`) stands in with an estimated map (`tools/rooms/prep_hero_plates.py`: sharp detail and the lower frame are near; the light is the brightest few per cent). Requested for every theme: `artreq_20261108-1500_room-layers-all`. |
| The main film (`art/splash/*.mp4`) | The pregenerated 1080p opening film, one at random per launch, before the theme's opening (`splash.js` filmPath, filmEnd; owner 2026-11-07). Never a theme's opening (rule 12a). |
| TitanScape (`audio/scape/`) | The sound engine for the backdrop and the room: recorded layers, placed in space, turned with the pan (`TitanScape.face`). |
| Recorded music (`audio/music/`) | The theme's own recordings (moods in `audio/music/manifest.json`); never generated music. |
| Added effects (`wings/fx/layers.js`) | Any effect in the library on top of a theme's photograph, chosen by the owner (TitanFX overlay presets; the paint swirl on `fx/kit.js` drawn over the photo). |
| TitanQuality (`wings/quality.js`) | The one quality setting (Low ... Max): the photo's render scale and how many parallax steps and particle depths it draws. Nothing lowers it on its own. |
| Old, kept only as a fallback | `js/motion-splash.js` (the still opening for Motion Calm, and when there is no WebGL2), `wings/fx/engine.js` (TitanFX: only for added effects and a sound scene's effect; a theme's own old screen effect no longer plays, since most painted a background over the photo). |

## 2. The slots every theme fills

1. **Photograph**: a full layer set from the art queue, or its own picture until then. Its entry in `wings/plate.js` `P`: the room's
   name, `pan0` (where an upright phone starts across the wide photo: on the subject), the colour of its light, and its live layers
   (dust, rain, snow, embers, bubbles, fireflies, leaves, rain slant, lamp flicker and its rate, shafts, aurora, caustics, lightning,
   depth and light strength, `light: 1` for a light theme, which is never dimmed, and `fd`, the front object's depth, when the full set
   is in). A new layer set: `python3 tools/rooms/prep_plates.py SRC ID room_` writes `art/rooms/ID/`, then add `fd` to its entry.
2. **The backdrop**: the photo behind the collection at 80 % brightness (a light theme at full), 24 frames a second, a slow drift, never
   more pixels than the photo has where it is shown (the crispness pass only in the room); a
   scroll moves the camera down the room and the mouse leans it a little (desktop). It holds still under the 1080p film (`html.ts-filmplay`),
   on a hidden tab, under an opaque shell (`TitanPlate.hold`, The Cabinet), and draws one still frame under Motion Calm or Off. On a
   renderer with no graphics chip (SwiftShader, llvmpipe: every frame there stalls the page) it draws once a second, the room and the
   opening at 15 a second; a real GPU is never downscaled. A theme change dips the old photo to dark and brings the new one
   up with a short push in (`TitanPlate.arrive`), so the page behind never flashes.
3. **The opening** (`wings/room.js` `engineOpening`, called by `splash.js` after the film or on its own): the splash goes transparent over
   the photograph, which comes up out of the dark as the camera pushes in from far and to one side (5.6 s); the light swells as the theme's
   name comes up at 55 %; then the app fades in over the same photo, which stays as the backdrop. If the photo is not up within 5 s the
   still opening (`js/motion-splash.js`) plays instead. Motion Calm and Off: the still opening.
4. **The room** (Step inside: `wings/room-shell.js` + `styles/room-shell.css`, loaded on first use): the same canvas at full brightness
   and full quality with the app hidden; drag pans across the photo (a flick glides), pinch or the wheel comes closer, a tap sends a
   ripple and a surge of light, the phone's tilt leans the camera. Panels: Look (Light, Particles, Depth, Film grain; saved per theme,
   the same values as the Atmosphere's Look > Effects), Effects (added ones), Sound (its soundscape and layers), Opening (replay the push
   in). ✕, Escape or Back: the photo goes back to being the backdrop.
5. **Sound**: the soundscape preset (`audio/scape/bridge.js` `THEME_SCAPE`) from recordings only, and the theme's recorded music.
6. **Art** (rule 9): picker card + hero, and the room's layer set, all from the image AIs, never rendered in the container.

## 3. The gate

- **CL-WORLD-02** (`tools/smoke/test_regress_cl_world_contract.py`, CI job `world` in `.github/workflows/smoke.yml`): every theme has
  its photograph and files; every theme boots on its photograph (phone: all 50; desktop: three) with one canvas, no code-built world, no
  old screen effect and no page errors; six theme switches leave one canvas; the room (full brightness, the app hidden with nothing
  showing through, a drag pans, Escape gives the backdrop back); the launch opening pushes into the photo and hands it to the app;
  Motion Off = one still frame.
- **GRK-P-02**: the film first, then the push into the theme's photograph, then the app.
- `tools/themes/framework_status.py --check` (smoke job): the status page matches the code.
- Everything else that already gates a theme still does (smoke, contrast, registry, GRK-Q-02, GRK-M-04).

## 4. Who does what

- **Claude**: the photograph engine, the shell, the gate, every merge; reviews every layer set at full size before it goes in, and tunes
  each theme's live layers on a phone.
- **Gemini, Grok, Muse** (art queue + homework): the layer sets (`artreq_20261108-1500_room-layers-all`, a claim file per room), and the
  sound hunt (`docs/agents/SOUND_HUNT.md`: music each theme lacks, field recordings for each room).

## 5. The plan, in order

- **A. Every theme a photograph** (done 2026-11-08): the photograph engine, the backdrop, the opening and the room for all 50; the code
  worlds off.
- **B. Every theme's own layer set**: as the art queue's sets come in, `prep_plates.py`, `fd` in its entry, the larger remakes of the
  first nine.
- **C. Tune each theme's live layers on a real phone** (light colour, particles, flicker), with the owner's notes.
- **D. Retire**: the world files in `fx/`, the openings kit and TitanFX's per-theme presets once nothing uses them (the main film stays).
