Text copy of the owner's live checklist (claude.ai/artifact/Aogoa4MfwCVPpck9xKUV8M), regenerated by Claude whenever the list changes. Item numbers never change. `Needs first` = prerequisites; `Unlocks` = what it makes possible. Copied 2026-10-08, build tr98.

## State of the union (2026-10-07, build tr98)

The one thing to be best in the world at: the most trustworthy record of a personal coin collection. Every fact shows where it came from, which AI or person said it, and how sure we are, and the owner can confirm it with one tap. Nobody does this for a private collection, and Titan already does most of it.

- **285** pieces in flips (279 coins, 6 tokens), 49 countries
- **$5,369** headline value, priced daily from real spot
- **208** pieces with a phone photo; 77 still need one, 14 have both sides
- **0** pro (Phase 2) photos so far
- **917 / 1775** album slots filled, all 33 albums read from your page photos
- **1** fact confirmed by you, out of 964 facts added since the ledger

### What is strong

- Trust machinery. Every fact has a certainty label and a history. Catalogue facts must cite the exact entry. Owner questions and Confirm buttons live in the app. Bad AI files are bounced with a line-numbered report.
- One pipeline, one master. Any AI drops a change file; one script checks it, merges it and rebuilds the site. 37 pipeline tests, a parity check against Grok's last ledger, and a 23-point phone + desktop smoke test (offline included) run on every deploy.
- Never silently broken. Daily prices, live-site check, smoke test and a daily backup zip all run on GitHub; Health shows a warning when any of them fails.
- Dad-readable. The Simple view answers “Do I have…?” and “What’s missing?” in big type, and the app works offline.

### What is holding it back

- Most added facts are still AI guesses. Of the 964 facts added since the ledger: 680 AI guess, 146 cited reference, 81 read from a photo, 56 need review, 1 confirmed by you. The labels are honest; the data is not yet proven.
- No measured accuracy. Five AIs contribute and we have never measured which one reads coins best (#4, #19, #55).
- Photos are the bottleneck. 77 coins have no photo, 194 have only one side, none have pro photos. Phase 2 cannot start until that is done, and only you can take them.
- The look is not decided. The design direction has been paused since Oct 1 (#30). A world-best app needs one confident look, not three prototypes.
- Safety net gaps. The Drive backup script was never installed (the only Drive copy is from Sept 28; GitHub holds everything), and the daily intake routine still has no Drive access.

### The next three moves, in order

- 1. Turn guesses into facts. Answer the 10 questions in the app (minutes); photograph the 77 missing coins, ordered by #54; #57 shows the finish line.
- 2. Measure the AIs. Run the bake-off (#4) and the two-AI pilot (#55), so every future fact comes from the reader that has earned it.
- 3. Pick the look. Choose or combine the prototypes (#30); then polish only on top of the chosen direction.
