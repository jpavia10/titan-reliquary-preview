# A Simple Guide to the Collection

This is the easy way to look at Joseph's coin collection on your phone or tablet. Big words. Short sentences. Nothing to break.

## How to get there

Open the Titan Reliquary app. On the first screen, tap the button that says **"Simple view."**

To leave, tap **"Back to the collection."**

## The four things you can do

### 1. Do I have...?

This is the one you will use most. It answers one question: **is this coin in the collection?**

- Type a year, a coin, or a country. Example: *1964 Kennedy*.
- Tap **Look**.
- It will say **Yes**, **Missing**, **Probably**, or **Not yet**.

If it says Yes, you will see a photo. Tap the photo to open the coin's full page.

**Try this:** type *Silver Eagle 2008* and tap Look.

### 2. What's missing?

This tells you which coins are still needed to finish each album set.

- You will see a row of buttons. Each one is a coin family, like Silver Eagles or Washington Quarters.
- Each button says how many are missing.
- Tap a family. It lists every album in that family, how many coins it has, and which years are still empty.

**Try this:** tap the Silver Eagles button. It shows exactly which years are missing.

### 3. At a glance

A few plain facts about the whole collection:

- How many coins and tokens, and how many countries they come from.
- How full the albums are.
- How much silver and gold is in it.
- What the collection is worth. (It is marked HOLD. Not for sale.)

It also says how sure the facts are. Some facts come from the coins themselves. Some are best guesses. It tells you which is which.

### 4. Questions

Sometimes a coin has a question only the real coin can answer, like a date that is hard to read.

- If there are questions waiting, this section appears near the top.
- It tells you how many there are and shows a button: **Answer the questions.**
- If there are no questions, this section hides itself. You will not see it.

## Three good habits

1. **The words are big on purpose.** If anything looks too small, tell Joseph and he will fix it.
2. **Everything here comes from the collection list.** If something looks wrong — a coin that should be there is not, or a number seems off — tell Joseph. He is the one who can fix the list.
3. **You cannot break anything.** This view only reads. It never changes or deletes anything. Tap freely.
